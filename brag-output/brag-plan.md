# Brag Plan: Parkmed Healthcare LLP — NABH Accreditation

## What is this?
A formal announcement film: Parkmed Healthcare LLP, Bengaluru, has received NABH accreditation as an allopathic clinic. Audience: corporate clients, enterprise partners, healthcare stakeholders, senior leadership.

## The angle
An annual-report spread brought to life. One continuous off-white sheet, a fixed editorial grid, a running head and folio that persist across all five sections, and typography doing all the work. The film never "performs"; it states a milestone with the composure of an established institution.

## Hook (first 2–3 seconds)
The original Parkmed logo, alone and precisely placed on the sheet, settling into position. The running head and hairlines draw in quietly around it: "this is an official communication."

## Key moments
- The headline "An Important Milestone in Quality Healthcare" set in a large editorial serif, revealed line by line from behind a mask.
- The accreditation record presented as a ruled editorial table: NABH Accreditation / Parkmed Healthcare LLP / Allopathic Clinic / Bengaluru, Karnataka.
- The commitment line in two measured lines: "Committed to Quality." / "Focused on Patient Safety."

## Outro
Logo returns with its tagline ("Healthcare Delivered", part of the artwork) and the line "Thank you for your continued trust." Held, then the film ends cleanly.

## User flow worth showing
None — this is a corporate announcement, not a product. The "real material" is the original logo artwork and the exact announcement wording supplied by Parkmed.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: "high-end corporate annual report / established healthcare group brand communication"
- Interpretation: 5 sections with long holds, one shared easing family, opacity and short vertical settles only. No zooms, spins, bounces, typewriter, glows, gradients, icons, stock imagery, particles or 3D.

## Format
- Primary: vertical — 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264
- Secondary: landscape — 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264 (re-composed on its own grid, not cropped)

## Duration: 21.0 s

## Visual identity (sampled from the supplied logo file)
- Background: off-white sheet `#F7F7F4`, white panels `#FFFFFF`
- Text: charcoal `#2B2C2E` for statements; logo charcoal `#4C4D4F` for secondary text
- Accents: Parkmed green `#1FB25A`, Parkmed blue `#0065B2` (exact logo values), used only for rules, labels and the two-tone divider (green then blue, echoing "Park" + "med")
- Display font: Newsreader (editorial serif, Light 300 / Regular 400) — SIL OFL
- Label/data font: IBM Plex Sans (400/500/600) — SIL OFL
- Logo: `assets/logo/parkmed-logo.png`, untouched original (1083×474, transparent), scaled uniformly only

## Wording (verbatim, nothing added)
- "An Important Milestone in Quality Healthcare"
- "Parkmed Healthcare LLP is proud to announce its NABH accreditation."
- "NABH ACCREDITATION" / "Parkmed Healthcare LLP" / "Allopathic Clinic" / "Bengaluru, Karnataka"
- "Committed to Quality. Focused on Patient Safety."
- "Thank you for your continued trust."
- Editorial furniture only: running head "Parkmed Healthcare LLP" and "NABH Accreditation", section folio 01–05, table labels "Organisation", "Scope", "Location". No grades, certificate numbers, dates, seals, or official marks.

## Share copy (draft)
Parkmed Healthcare LLP, Bengaluru, is now NABH accredited as an allopathic clinic. Committed to quality, focused on patient safety — thank you for your continued trust.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, low bed under the film; intentional restraint
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (ende.app, CC BY 4.0, commercial use allowed) — the steadiest bundled track
- Treatment: volume ~0.45 (≈ -23 LUFS integrated), 1.2 s fade-in, fade-out over the final ~1.8 s so the film ends in silence on the held frame
- Music cue guidance: preset read (109.96 BPM). Strong cues 9.29 s (accreditation title) and beat 18.02 s (final logo) are used as soft locks; everything else on natural timing for readability
- Audio-reactive treatment: none — constant movement and "breathing" elements conflict with the brief
- SFX posture: none. A corporate announcement should not click or chime
- Deliver a silent variant alongside

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Opening — 0.0–4.0 s
Off-white sheet. Original logo centred on the grid (upper-middle), fades in with an 18 px settle. Running head hairline and folio "01" draw in around it. Deliberate hold. Logo lifts out softly at 3.5 s.
Sequential: logo → furniture. Transition: soft, content out then in (no double exposure) → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Announcement — 4.0–9.0 s
Label "Announcement" (blue, tracked caps). Headline in Newsreader Light, revealed line by line from masks. Two-tone rule draws left to right. Subline in Plex Sans on a pale panel. Headline settled ~3.3 s, subline ~2.1 s.
Transition: soft → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Accreditation — 9.0–14.0 s
White record panel with ruled rows. Title "NABH Accreditation" (serif). Three rows arrive 0.35 s apart and hold together for ~3 s: Organisation — Parkmed Healthcare LLP; Scope — Allopathic Clinic; Location — Bengaluru, Karnataka. Secondary in scale to Scene 2's headline.
Transition: soft → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Commitment — 14.0–18.0 s
"Committed to Quality." then "Focused on Patient Safety." — two serif lines, second line 0.4 s later, two-tone rule between. Held ~2.5 s.
Transition: soft → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Close — 18.0–21.0 s
Original logo (with its tagline) returns centred; "Thank you for your continued trust." beneath in Plex Sans. Settled by ~19.2 s, held to 21.0 s. Music fades out; picture holds to the last frame.

**Music mood:** calm, steady, low.
**Audio summary:** a quiet bed rises in, sits under the typography and resolves to silence on the held final frame.
